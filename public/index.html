Doodlechain

The drawing telephone game for your group chat

Someone writes “a cat doing taxes”. The next person draws it. The next guesses the drawing. Everyone plays whenever they have a minute, then the whole chain is revealed.

  • One link in the chat, no app, no sign-up
  • Turns take a minute, on your phone
  • Same link for a new round every day

    How it works

    1. 1

      Send the link

      Create a game and drop the link in your WhatsApp, iMessage, Messenger or Discord group. Friends join with a nickname.

    2. 2

      Draw or guess

      Each turn you see only the step before yours: a phrase to draw, or a drawing to name. Several chains run at once, so nobody waits.

    3. 3

      Watch it fall apart

      When everyone has played (or at the time you chose), the reveal plays each chain from start to finish. Save one as an image to share.

    Questions

    Does everyone need to be online at the same time?

    No. That is the point. Each person plays whenever they open the link: on the bus, at lunch, before bed. The chains move on as people play.

    What if someone never plays?

    Pick a reveal time when you create the game, or reveal early as the host. The game never waits forever.

    Do I need an account or an app?

    No. Open the link, type a nickname, play. Your browser remembers you on that device.

    Can we play again tomorrow?

    Yes. After the reveal anyone can start the next round on the same link, so the group keeps one link in the chat.

    Who can see the drawings?

    Only people with your game’s link. There is no public feed. The host can remove any step after the reveal or delete the whole game. Inactive games are deleted after 90 days.
