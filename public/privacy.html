Doodlechain

Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

Doodlechain is a drawing telephone game you play through a link. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What we store

  • Your game: its name, the nicknames of players, the phrases, guesses and drawings (as pen strokes), and when each was made.
  • A device key: when you join, your browser keeps a random key in its local storage so it remembers you. We store only a one-way hash of it.
  • Rate limits: a counter per IP address for one hour, to stop abuse. It is deleted when the hour is up.

We do not ask for your name, email or phone number, we do not use cookies, and we do not sell or share data. There are no analytics or third-party scripts.

Who can see it

Only people who have your game’s link. During a round each player sees only the one step before theirs. After the reveal, everyone in the game sees the chains. There is no public gallery.

Deleting

The host can remove any step after the reveal, remove players, or delete the whole game with everything in it. Players can leave a game. Games with no activity for 90 days are deleted automatically.

Where it runs

Doodlechain runs on Cloudflare (Pages, Functions and D1 database). Cloudflare processes requests to serve the site.

Contact

Questions or a request to delete something: open an issue at github.com/janwan2003/draw-telephone/issues.

About: Doodlechain is a free drawing telephone game for group chats.
